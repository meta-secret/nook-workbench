# Deliver independent identities and recognizable passkey cards

## Outcome

Devices & access can create and switch among independently protected local
identities. Add identity opens the existing browser-protection ceremony. The
passkey card now leads with its full user name and shows a safe credential
fingerprint, storage keeper, first-recorded time, and last-use time. Apps are
subordinate context, while the raw app identifier is available only under
Advanced.

## Product changes

- Removed the bottom-of-page Prepare this browser action and reused that
  ceremony from Add identity.
- Added transactional creation, selection, persistence, recovery, rollback,
  and removal for multiple local identity keyrings.
- Removed legacy access-evidence and browser-diagnostic panels.
- Kept passkey rename with the passkey and shared the recognizable card across
  list, graph, and mobile presentations.
- Localized the revised experience in English and Russian and updated the
  owning Devices & access specification.

## Decisions

- A passkey or PIN is the user-facing protection object. An app key remains an
  internal implementation detail, represented as an app relationship and an
  advanced identifier only.
- Missing credential, keeper, creation, or last-use evidence is shown as an
  explicit unknown state rather than inferred from browser diagnostics.
- Rollback and legacy projections are deleted only after transactional
  ownership checks confirm they belong to the identity being retired.

  focused issue and continues under its existing owner.
