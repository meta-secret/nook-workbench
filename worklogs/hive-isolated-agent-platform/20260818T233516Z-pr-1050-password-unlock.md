## Implementation problems

- Website lock clears identity_private_key. Paired handoff then demanded an
  authorizing app key, so every post-lock unlock failed even when the companion
  session was still Unlocked.
- Earlier extension cycles failed on popup discovery, waitForEvent URL
  filters, and a website passkey overlay after the first failed adopt.
- The e2e helper scraped context.pages() after lock, when no chrome-extension
  page remained, and aborted before the owned popup opened.
- A concurrent $effect adopt raced begin_extension_identity_handoff and then
  returned instead of waiting for a fresh companion nonce.

## Decisions

- Session-unlock enrollment verifies store_id and clears pending without
  directory enroll.
- Keep retrying paired adoption while the companion reports Unlocked or Locked.
- Do not open a website passkey overlay in those companion states.
- Land unique product bits on one surviving branch. Leave cache-contract and
  bake-context edits out.
- Do not re-arm isolated Main-repair workers.
