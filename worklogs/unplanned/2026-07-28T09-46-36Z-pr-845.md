split into a 473-line extension lifecycle suite, a 556-line passkey/session
recovery suite, and a 266-line shared runtime harness.

## Progress

- Separated passkey and offscreen-session recovery scenarios from extension

  activation, and popup helpers into one shared harness.

- Keep browser/runtime mechanics in a narrow shared test capability module.
