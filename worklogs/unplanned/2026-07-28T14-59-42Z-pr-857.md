## Outcome

Split the 2,477-line authored landing document by architectural responsibility instead of mechanically cutting it:

- semantic document shell in `index.html` (563 lines)
- landing behavior and lifecycle in `src/landing/main.js`
- translation catalog in `src/landing/messages.js`
- shell/hero, vault visualization, product-section, and responsive style modules
- behavior-focused recorded demo for locale, theme, extension install, and cryptography interaction

The extracted site bundle now uses mount-relative assets. This preserves the production-root deployment and the assembled `/site/` PR preview; the recorded demo exposed and prevented the otherwise-static preview regression.
