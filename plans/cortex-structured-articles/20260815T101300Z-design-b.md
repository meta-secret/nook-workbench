# Migrate structured design documents B

## Interpreted request

Complete the universal article grammar across the design family. Make complex
architecture, state transitions, invariants, and execution order easy to scan
without reducing necessary explanation or changing authority.

## Requirements

- Preserve architecture, security boundaries, terminology, and exact commands.
- Use ordered lists only where implementation, ceremony, or recovery order
  changes the outcome.
- Use bullets and nesting for peer invariants, alternatives, ownership, and
  state-dependent behavior.
- Keep rationale and tradeoff analysis as prose in clearly bounded articles.
- Synchronize document maps and relationships.
- Remove the final seven design-document paths from the legacy ledger.

- This is a Markdown-only structural migration.
- Product specs, references, execution plans, and root documents remain for
  later slices.
- Runtime, product, and Workbench behavior do not change.
